# Mid Intelligence Pack 2

This batch promotes eight additional mid heroes from conservative baseline profiles to detailed coaching profiles:

- Leshrac
- Death Prophet
- Kunkka
- Necrophos
- Outworld Destroyer
- Pangolier
- Primal Beast
- Templar Assassin

Each profile includes at least two concrete build plans and three named power spikes with level/item triggers, stage curves, benchmarks, action bias, vulnerabilities, and player-facing recommendations.

## Strategic separation

The pack is intentionally not a copied caster template:

- Templar Assassin is calibrated around Roshan, armor reduction, and physical objective conversion.
- Death Prophet prioritizes Exorcism-based tower and objective windows.
- Pangolier and Primal Beast emphasize initiation and teamfight entry.
- Necrophos and Leshrac emphasize sustained front-line fighting with different survival risks.
- Kunkka emphasizes area control and damage reduction.
- Outworld Destroyer emphasizes positioning and protected damage uptime.

## Calibration boundary

Values remain prototype coaching calibration. They must be reviewed against live recordings and the active balance patch before release calibration is considered final.

Detailed profile coverage after this batch: **48 / 127 heroes**. The remaining **79** heroes stay on conservative baseline profiles.
