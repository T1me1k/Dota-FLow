# Session Coach copy guidelines

Use calm, optional language: “consider,” “may,” “in your history,” “not enough data,” and “the decision remains yours.” Describe reported states and observable correlations, never identity or character. Keep a recommendation to a title, concise explanation, at most three reasons, confidence/data sufficiency, and an optional safer alternative.

Do not claim certainty, clinical validity, causation, dependency, mental-health status, or guaranteed wins/losses. Never shame the player or say they cannot play. Engine copy lives in the centralized English copy contract; enums never appear as primary player-facing text.
